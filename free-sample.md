## Method and provenance

- Collection is limited to the public HTTP(S) listing indexes in `sources.json`; attachments are not bulk-downloaded.
- Dedupe uses normalized tender/reference numbers first, then conservative title/agency similarity; merged observations are retained as provenance metadata.
- Table headers are mapped by label with a positional fallback for known public page shapes.
- A failed or empty source uses its last parsed listing cache and is visibly marked; it never silently becomes fresh data.
- Run mode: `collect`. Raw fetch logs, caches and (for live runs) HTML snapshots are stored under `data/`; the dated issue is under `archive/`.

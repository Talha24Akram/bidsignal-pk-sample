Signal, not noise

The narrow layer between an official portal and a bid team.

BidSignal keeps the source link and removes the busywork: collecting, deduplicating, classifying, scoring, change-watching and archiving the opportunities most likely to fit an ICT supplier.

01 · Collect

Public indexes, once a day

Federal PPRA/EPADS keyword views, PPRA procurement plans, MoITT, NITB and Ministry of Planning listing pages. No login, paywall bypass or attachment firehose.

02 · Triage

Fit, urgency and movement

Whole-term category evidence, deadline urgency, displayed status, source confidence and new/updated/unchanged labels with planned demand kept separate.

03 · Verify

Every fact has a provenance trail

Open the originating public listing, inspect the raw field values, and distinguish source facts from the product's keyword-based inference.

Buyer-controlled setup

Preview the fit before recurring access.

The included profile package lets a buyer edit categories, terms, brands, regions and agencies, validate the file locally, and preview matches with reasons and exclusions. It accepts no passwords, tokens, payment data, portal logins or attachment requests.
